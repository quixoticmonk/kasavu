# Kasavu

A Kiro Crew theme named for the gold-bordered handloom cloth of Kerala, India,
and drawn from the state's wider visual culture.

* **"Kasavu Morning"**: the handloom cream and gold zari border (kasavu / kara) of the mundum neriyathum.
* Light-only: the pack format requires a `dark` palette, so the `dark` block in `variables.json` and `topbar/dark.html` are copies of the light ones. Dark mode looks the same as light.

Status colors follow the panchavarna (five colours) of Kerala temple murals and the Kathakali makeup code:

* ok: Pachcha green (noble characters)
* warn: Minukku saffron (gentle characters)
* danger: red laterite, the mural pigment, and the Thaadi red of villains
* info: monsoon and backwater blue

## What it adds

* Kasavu bands (a woven gold band with a pin line) on the top bar, the composer and the session sidebar, plus a gold edge on code blocks.
* കസവ് (Kasavu) wordmark in the bottom-right corner.
* Loader art: Kathakali Pachcha face, Kalivilakku lamp, coconut palm, chundan vallam (snake boat).
* Welcome icon: Kathakali face. Tab icon: Kalivilakku flame.

## Level 2 experience

* Top bar: an animated kasavu border along the very top of the window, with a slow zari shimmer (still under reduced motion).
* No theme sounds: Kiro Crew's own notification sounds play.
